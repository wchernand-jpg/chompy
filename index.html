Hello from Chompy

Chompy is a small friendly dinosaur with a red backpack and a lot of questions. His favorite word is "why".

Each week he goes somewhere new with a friend who knows the way. One week it is the night sky. Another week it is the kitchen, the ocean or the garden.

What we make

  • Sing-along songs that teach one true thing at a time.
  • Short stories with a gentle ending.
  • Move-along songs for clapping, stomping, jumping and spinning.
  • Classic nursery rhymes.
  • Little stories that teach a few Spanish words.

How we make it

Every fact is checked before a video is made. The pace is calm. Each video gives a child one clear thing to learn or do, and it is best watched with a grown-up who joins in.
