Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version: we do not collect personal information from the people who visit this website or watch our videos.

This website

  • There are no accounts, forms or sign-ups.
  • We do not use cookies, ads or tracking.
  • The company that hosts the site may keep ordinary server records, such as the internet address a page was requested from. We do not use those records to identify anyone.

Children

Our videos are made for young children. We do not collect any information from children, and this website does not ask them for any.

Our upload tool

We use a small program of our own to send our finished videos to our own TikTok account as drafts. Only we use it.

When we sign in to TikTok with it, the program receives:

  • Our own account's id and display name.
  • Permission to upload videos to that account as drafts.

That sign-in is kept on our own computer. It is not shared with anyone and it is not sold. The program does not read, collect or store information about any other TikTok user.

We can remove the program's access at any time in the TikTok app's settings.

Other platforms

When you watch Chompy on TikTok, YouTube or another service, that service collects information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about individual viewers from them.

Messages

If you send us a message, we use it only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
